05 — FAQ

Questions

Do I need experience?+

No. Module 01 starts from zero. Already experienced? Step in at A Style of Your Own or Workflow.

Which tools do we use?+

The course is tool-independent: the principles work in any image model. The demos use the latest generation of image and video models, plus Claude for the workflow.

How long do I keep access?+

Forever. New lessons and module updates are included.

Can I use what I make commercially?+

Yes — module 06 is entirely about it: publishing, clients and rights.
